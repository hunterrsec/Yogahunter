About Us

Traditional Wisdom for Modern Transformation

At Polem Beach Yoga, our vision is to cultivate conscious teachers through a profound journey of spiritual evolution.

RYS 200Yoga Alliance

Our Story & Vision

We bridge ancient yogic wisdom with modern functional teaching methodologies, providing a sanctuary for true inner transformation. Our approach emphasizes individual attention, philosophical depth, and functional anatomy, ensuring every student develops the awareness required to guide others with authenticity and grace.

Polem Beach Yoga is a premier RYS 200 Yoga Alliance USA certified school, providing elite 200-hour yoga teacher trainings and transformative retreats within our peaceful coastal sanctuary in Goa.

Beyond our signature programs, we invite global wellness leaders to bring their own unique retreats to our shores — gathering your community at our premier Goa beach venue with a specialized shala for profound movement, deep restoration, and authentic connection.

Teaching Methodology

Bridging Traditional Yogic Wisdom & Functional Anatomy

Our teaching methodology is a continuous journey of transformation.

Our approach bridges ancient yogic wisdom with modern functional anatomy, ensuring a safe and profound practice. We prioritize individual attention, specialized teaching methodology, and deep philosophy to cultivate inner awareness.

By exploring the connection between asana, pranayama, and meditation, we provide students with the inner tools needed to become conscious and visionary teachers.

  • Individual attention in every session
  • Specialized teaching methodology
  • Deep philosophy and inner awareness
  • Connection of asana, pranayama & meditation
  • Conscious, visionary teaching
