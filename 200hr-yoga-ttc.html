Full Syllabus

Course Curriculum

Six modules uniting ancient maps with modern functional teaching methodology.

01

Asana Alchemy

Clinical breakdown of traditional Hatha and dynamic Vinyasa, mastering the geometry of the soul through the body.

02

Functional Architecture

Functional studies of biomechanics and fascia, bridging ancient safety with modern athletic precision.

03

The Path of Teaching

Immersion into methodology, verbal cues, and hands-on adjustments to build your confidence as a guide.

04

Breath & Stillness

Mastering Pranayama and the science of meditation to regulate the nervous system and calm the mind.

05

Ancient Maps

Studying the Sutras and Gita not as historical text, but as a lived philosophy for modern consciousness.

06

Sacred Sequencing

Designing soul-led flows that follow the inner rhythm of nature and the laws of intelligent movement.
