Is This Retreat For You?

Guidance & Practice

I believe yoga is a path to inner balance, best practiced where nature's rhythm is most felt. My goal is for guests to leave feeling lighter, clearer, and deeply rested.

This retreat is for you if...

  • You seek a personal, small-group atmosphere
  • You want to practice yoga right by the ocean
  • You appreciate fresh, vegetarian Ayurvedic meals
  • You are looking for a flexible yet supported stay

This retreat is not for you if...

  • You prefer large, commercial hotel resorts
  • You are looking for a party-centric vacation
  • You are not interested in vegetarian or healthy food
  • You prefer crowded, busy beaches
